# Security policy

Please report a suspected vulnerability in any Figentra product or repository **privately** to
**info@figentra.com** with "Security" in the subject. Do not open a public issue.

Include what is affected, how to reproduce it and the impact you observed. We acknowledge reports within
three working days and keep you informed until the issue is resolved.

Figentra Technologies FZ-LLC, Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates.
